FAQ

Is StudySprint an app?

Not yet. V2 is a digital study system delivered as a printable/downloadable resource. A future version could become a web app.

Can I use AI with it?

Yes. The included prompts are designed to help you explain topics, practise, generate flashcards and review mistakes. Always verify important information.

Do I need to be good at studying already?

No. The system is designed to give you a clear starting point and a repeatable weekly routine.
